Japanese Spider Crabs can grow a leg span of 18'and weigh as much as 42 lb. This must be a baby. I thought they might grow proportional to the tank size, but apparently that's a myth. Fish don't.

To accommodate growth, the crab moults periodically. It takes several weeks to separate itself from the old shell, discarding esophagus, stomach lininig and part of the intestine in the process, to be regenerated later. Until the new shell hardens, the crab is vulnerable to predation, including by its own kind.

Manta rays visit cleaning stations, where fish congregate to have parasites removed. The station may be at a coral reef, a large clump of seaweed, or simply and agreed-upon location. The ray opens its mouth to signal that it wants a cleaning. Cleaner shrimp and fish such as wrasses and gobies (which lack a gas bladder, are not bouyant, and so must remain on the bottom.

Jellyfish in the Medusa phase. This is the breeding phase. Medusas release sperm and eggs from their mouths into the water, where they combine to form ciliated swimming larvae. After a few days, the larvae settle to the sea floor and grow into stalk-like polyps that have a mouth and tentacles, and feed on plankton. The polpys can reproduce asexually by budding, or sexually by turning into a medusa, depending on the species.

The Banded Pipefish (upper right) lives in tropical tide pools and other shallows. It grows to a maximum length of 7". To reproduce, the female lays her eggs into the male's brood pouch. The male later gives birth.

The Leafy Seadragon is the emblem of the state of South Australia. The pods are not used for propulsion; they simply make the fish look like seaweed. It's so unique that it's the only member of its genus, Phycodurus.
