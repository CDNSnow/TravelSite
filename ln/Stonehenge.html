The Henge
The stones seemed to be huddling inward, saying "We have a secret, and w'ere not telling you. We're waiting for the right ones to appear..those who live in subservience to nature."

There was actually a fair crowd here.  The path around the Henge is as artfully laid out as a Zen garden to give you the impression of being alone.
