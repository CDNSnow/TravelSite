-=Factoids=-

  • Appetizers are entrees. Entrees are mains.
  • Prezzies are presents, but prezzos are dog & ponies.
  • The kind of mangos we get in the US are called "cooking mangos".
  • The Australian national religion aspires to turn signal heaven through the religious use of signals. This is especially useful in a town where 10% of the intersections are roundabouts. Every American should be sent here for driver's ed.
  • The leisure ethic has everyone so laid back that they stop if you're a meter from the kerb, but heading that way. (Don't try this in Sydney though.)
  • Peter and I were talking down the street when a drunk called Peter a fucking American. He's Irish.
  • Will try to record the Puking Crows of Death. If I were a sound mixer, I'd find lots of uses for them.
  • The front page had a article about Arab gangs attacking locals at a Sydney beach every weekend. The locals sent SMS messages and fought back last weekend. There'll be a massive police presence this weekend because SOMEBODY'S FACE GOT BLOODIED.
  • Last night someone said "I've got a friend in New Jersey. Is that a city or a state?"
  • Also been told "Since you're in the DC meditation group, you must go to Encinitas for retreats all the time". (Yeah, like you go to Perth, lady.)
  • At parties, men collect in one corner and women in another. On the way home, my buddy calls me mate, pats me on the shoulder and tells me "silly woman" stories about his wife. Slang for wife (based on Cockney rhyming slang) is "trouble and strife".
  • Whether I'm in Canberra or DC, it'll be 32 degees at Christmas.

This is the end of my 10-week tryout. I want more Australia, and the Australian government wants more me. The paperwork isn't through, but the plan is that I'll return in Jan through June (and probably the rest of the year in the end).

I was in a comfortable rut at home, and I'm like a kid with a new box of toys here. It does SO much for quality of life when everything from friends to kickboxing to any kind of restaraunt to swimming lessons is within a 20 minute drive from home or work at any time of day. The brilliant blue weather and gums remind me of what LA was like when I was tiny.
